# DozyPal Privacy Policy

DozyPal is a free, open-source app made for fun. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

## On your phone only

- **Accessibility Service**, limited to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. DozyPal gets notified when you scroll inside those four apps, and checks on-screen labels such as "Reels", "Shorts" and "Sponsored" to decide whether you moved to a new reel. It does not read messages or comments, and it does not save or send anything it sees.
- **Private app storage** for daily reel counts per app (kept up to 400 days for reports), your name and avatar choice, and your settings. Your name is never uploaded.

## Scroll Battles (optional)

Nothing is uploaded unless you open Scroll Battles and join. If you do:

- **Sign-in:** either Google sign-in or an anonymous account, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. With Google sign-in, Firebase knows your Google account; DozyPal does not show it to anyone.
- **What is stored** in Google Cloud Firestore (asia-south1, Mumbai): the nickname you choose, your avatar (male/female), your friend code, today's date, today's reel counts per app, and your friends list.
- **Who can see it:** only you and the friends you add (or who add you with your code). Nobody can browse or search all users.
- **Deleting:** tap **Leave Scroll Battles** to delete your profile, friend code and friendships from the server. Anonymous accounts are deleted too.

## What DozyPal does NOT do

- No ads, analytics or trackers.
- It does not watch any app except Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
- It never uploads your reel history, your real name, or anything from your screen.
